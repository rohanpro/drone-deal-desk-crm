# Deal Desk: thinking doc (draft for the Google Doc)

## What it does, and for whom
This is a Monday-morning deal desk for the CSM who has just inherited two drone-dock accounts, and for the manager they report to. It covers the Eastern Counties Police Alliance (£350k a year, renews 28 Feb 2027) and Aldermoor Industrial Group (€140k a year, renews 31 Jan 2027).

For each account it answers five questions:
- What is the book really worth?
- Who holds the power?
- What do we do first?
- What is about to go wrong?
- What don't we know?

Every number links to the id it came from. It reads live from the Account Quest MCP, notices new events by itself, and writes its #1 move and any new red risk back with `write_note`.

**What it found:**
- **A:** Only £270k of the £350k renewal is backed by usage. Westmere (18 docks) and Metro Central (9) fly. Tarn Valley and Norwold (8 docks, £80k) have flown no live missions in 6 months.
- **A:** The champion (Rhys Calder) leaves in January, before the renewal. Karen Mbeki needs written answers this week ahead of a 5 Dec public event, and the two docks with 17 battery warnings each are among the busiest.
- **B:** Only 4 of 14 docks fly regularly. The "€1.2M saved" figure has no working behind it. Group Finance asked for a per-plant quote on 8 Oct and nobody answered. The person who decides the renewal (Nadia Rahman, per the debrief) has never been met.

## How it decides: the rules
- **Value only what usage backs.**
  - Price per dock = contract ÷ docks.
  - A: docks at forces with any live missions in the verified force log count as backed.
  - B: docks averaging 4+ flights a month over the last 3 months count as backed.
  - Won upsells count. Open offers do not.
  - Claims are listed apart with what they would imply, and are never added.
- **Power:**
  - Decider and payer come from the debrief.
  - Users, blockers and champions come from facts and paperwork.
  - "Never met" is shown as a risk in its own right.
- **Moves:** ranked by log(value at stake) × urgency × time pressure. They are generated by rules that fire on the data, for example:
  - A "this week" request → reply by Friday.
  - A champion leaving → get it agreed now.
  - A debrief blocker → its specific unblocking move.
  - An ownerless request → assign it.
  - A never-met decider → get an introduction.

  Every move has an owner from the FlytBase team list and a date inside its deadline.
- **Risks:** each one carries a "turns bad" date, for example:
  - The end of the week, for a request asked "this week".
  - January, when the champion leaves.
  - Term end minus 30 days, for renewal paperwork.
  - 90 days of silence, for people going cold.
- **Gaps:** taken from what the profile says is not on file, unread tickets, claims, and deals the debrief knows that discovery missed. Each gap names who could fill it.
- **Data hygiene:** the force log has 4 date formats, 3–4 spellings per force, a duplicate row, a partial re-send and a missing month. Each fix is shown, and a missing month is treated as unknown, not zero.

## What it does not do yet
- It cannot map docks to forces or plants. The install sheets and list are not on file, so per-dock usage is not split by force.
- It does not read the contents of FB-10412. The link to the Tarn Valley DPIA is labelled as likely, not fact.
- Move ranking is a transparent heuristic, not a trained model. Debrief values (USD game scores) are shown, not counted.
- There is no login. Writing from the UI is guarded only by an optional `WRITE_KEY`.
- It has no forecasting beyond the dated risks.

## How I used AI
I used Claude (Claude Code) to do the following:
- Read every tab of the brief and every record in both paperwork packs.
- Design the sync to fit one MCP call a minute.
- Write the engine, the UI and the MCP endpoint.
- Check the result: a citation-integrity check that fails if any id does not resolve, plus a browser test of every view.

The judgement calls are encoded as readable rules in `src/analyze.mjs`, for example what counts as backed, who owns what, and what is a claim.
