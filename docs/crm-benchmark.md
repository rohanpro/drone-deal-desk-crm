# What the top 10 B2B CRMs do well, and what Flightdeck takes from each

Source: product knowledge of each vendor's current sales/CS editions (late 2026). The goal was not to clone any of them. It was to find the patterns that make a CRM *easy to act on* for a mixed team, then apply them to a renewal-and-expansion business selling drone-in-a-box SaaS.

| # | CRM | What it is best at | Pattern we adopted | Where it lives in Flightdeck |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Salesforce Sales Cloud** | Opportunity stages with exit criteria, forecast categories, Chatter on records, Einstein next-best-action | Stages with clear "to move forward" criteria. Forecast split into **Commit / Best case / Pipeline**. Chat on every account. | Pipeline board, Forecast card, Team chat |
| 2 | **HubSpot Sales + Service Hub** | Clean record page: activity timeline, tickets next to deals, email templates, AI assistant (Breeze) | **One timeline** of calls, emails, tickets and alerts, with the original attached. **Tickets** next to deals. **Ready-to-send drafts**. | Account › History, Tickets, "How to do it" drafts, Ask Flightdeck |
| 3 | **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales** | Relationship analytics and org charts, "who's gone quiet" warnings | **Buying committee map** with warm/neutral/guarded colours. **Going cold** warnings. | Account › People, Risks |
| 4 | **Pipedrive** | Colourful kanban, deal "rotting" when untouched, activity-first workflow | **Colour-coded kanban**. Deals flagged when stuck. Home page = **today's tasks**. | Pipeline, Today |
| 5 | **Zoho CRM** | Gamification (Motivator), role-based views, Blueprint process guidance | **Team points and streaks**. **Role switcher** so each person sees their own work. | Today (team momentum), role picker |
| 6 | **Freshsales (Freshworks)** | Deal and contact scores, Freddy AI insights, simple UI for small teams | **Plain-English health score** with the reasons behind it. No jargon on screen. | Account health, Summary |
| 7 | **Gainsight** (customer success) | Health scorecards, CTAs with **playbooks**, renewal management | **Health = adoption + relationships + access to decision makers + support + responsiveness**. Every action has a **playbook** (steps, owner, draft). | Health, Plan › "How to do it" |
| 8 | **Clari / Gong** (revenue intelligence) | MEDDICC scorecards, deal risk signals, forecast confidence | **MEDDPICC scorecard** per deal, each letter backed by evidence or marked unknown. | Deal card › Qualification |
| 9 | **Close** | Speed for reps: inbox-style task list, calling, sequences | **One-click actions**: mark done, discuss, copy draft, write back to the source system. | Plan, Today |
| 10 | **Attio / monday sales CRM** | Modern, minimal UI, collaborative comments, flexible boards | **Calm visual design**, progressive disclosure ("show more"), comments threaded per deal. | Whole UI, chat threads |

## Sales cycles and frameworks built in

| Framework | Used for | How it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **B2B pipeline stages** (Prospect → Discover → Qualify → Prove value → Proposal → Commit → Closed) | Expansion and new deals | Pipeline board columns. Each deal shows what is needed to move forward. |
| **Customer lifecycle "bow-tie"** (Onboard → Adopt → Expand → Renew) | Existing customers | Lifecycle bar on each account |
| **MEDDPICC** (Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition) | Qualifying every deal honestly | Scorecard on every deal: green = proven, amber = partly known, red = unknown |
| **Forecast categories** (Commit / Best case / Pipeline) | Telling the manager what will land | Forecast card |
| **Mutual action plan** | Renewals with many signers | Dated steps in each renewal play |
| **SPIN / Challenger** | How to run the conversation | Talk tracks inside each play (questions to ask, insight to teach) |
| **Health scoring** (Gainsight-style) | Early warning on renewals | Account health with its five reasons |

## Design rules we took from the benchmark
1. **Home answers "what do I do today?"** (Pipedrive, Close). It does not show a data dump.
2. **One plain sentence before any number** (Freshsales, HubSpot).
3. **Colour means one thing everywhere:** green good, amber watch, red act now (Gainsight).
4. **Every claim has its receipt one click away**, but the receipt stays hidden until asked for (HubSpot timeline attachments).
5. **Each role sees its own work first** (Zoho, Salesforce app pages).
6. **Conversation lives next to the record** (Chatter, Attio comments).
